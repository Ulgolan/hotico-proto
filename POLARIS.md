GOAL
Design and ship the desktop surface of hotico-proto: a fully
art-directed desktop experience for homepage + areola — founded
on a desktop design system that every current and future page
inherits — for which today's mobile proto remains the untouched,
ratified collapse. Proto mode: the artifact is a mold, not a
monument, and doubles as a production-grade specification.

AUDIENCE / BENEFICIARY
Primary: the research-mode visitor — patients weighing a
paramedical decision and professionals evaluating formation —
for whom desktop is the credibility and trust surface.
Secondary: Alexa — her brand composed at full scale; ratifies
anything touching her brand call.
Tertiary: ACP — desktop as his authored design-authority
artifact — and any future builder (webdev or Hands), for whom
the proto + system doc is the consumable build spec.

NON-NEGOTIABLES
1. MOBILE CHANGES ONLY BY RULING — mobile canon (≤767px) alters
   only by explicit Commander ratification, never as desktop
   side-effect. Ignition keys declare mobile blast radius; phone
   spot-check per merge; full mobile walk at milestones.
2. THE EYE GATES EVERY MERGE, PRE-MERGE — Vercel preview
   (toggle = pre-flight) or local branch serve as fallback.
   Graded at 1440px primary; 1280 + 1920 checked for grace.
   No route to the eye = no merge.
3. BRAND IS BEDROCK, COMPOSITION IS MOLD — Canon v1.1 and
   honest facades frozen except by Alexa-level ruling. Layout,
   hierarchy, motion re-pourable; any desktop text-length
   change is a named ratification, never a design freebie.
